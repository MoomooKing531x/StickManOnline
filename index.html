P2 ←→ move  ↑ jump  ↓ crouch  R-Shift dash  L block  O punch  I kick  P grab/throw  ↑+O uppercut  ↓+I sweep kick
A pencil sword artist lurks in the paper background and every so often sketches a weapon (pencil sword, sword, katana, hammer or the Dark Lord sword, max 4 per round). Once the sketch is finished it fills in with colour and drops: pointed ones stick in the floor, so kick one to pop it loose, then grab it mid-air with grab/throw. Stunned or dizzy fighters keep their weapon, and whoever stands next to them can press grab/throw to rip it away and knock them back. A K.O. does not end the round: the loser flashes away and the pencil sketches them back in (most kills when time runs out wins the round). Armed punches chain down, side, poke, rising and overhead slashes; grab/throw again to hurl it point-first: hold up for an upward throw, up or down plus a direction for diagonals, or crouch to just drop it. Crouching dodges punches but not kicks. A close dodge (duck, jump or dash) opens a slow-motion bubble around you; block right as a hit lands for a PARRY. Jump over or dash through your opponent to switch sides; you face whichever way you move. Tap punch twice for a jab–cross combo. Up + punch is an uppercut that launches; down + kick is a low 360° sweep. Jump a sweep and hit the sweeper before they recover and they fly off knocked out; jump on a fighter lying down to stomp them. Get your block hit 3 times in a row and your guard breaks: you're dizzy and wide open for a couple of seconds. In 1 player mode, both key sets work. PAD stick/D-pad move  A jump  X punch  Y kick  B dash  LB/LT block  RB/RT grab / catch / throw  (menu: A = 1P, X = 2P)
TOUCH left thumb = stick, right thumb = buttons (landscape is best)
PARRY a flying weapon (tap block right as it arrives) to freeze it mid-air, then hit R / RB / GRAB to catch it! HOW TO WIN
First to 3 KOs wins. No timer. A KO'd fighter is redrawn by the pencil and respawns. COMBAT
Tap block right as a hit lands to PARRY. Three blocked hits in a row cause a guard break. Sweeps can only be blocked by crouching + blocking (S+F). Crouch under high attacks, jump over sweeps. WEAPONS
The pencil draws a weapon every so often. Press R / RB / GRAB near one to pick it up, again to throw it (hold up/down to aim). Parry a flying weapon to freeze it mid-air, then press R to catch it. If you already hold one, the parry reflects it back. Grab a weapon from a stunned or dizzy enemy to disarm them. ONLINE
1V1 QUEUE matches you with the next player on the game server. HOST PARTY gives a 4-letter code to share; choose GAME SERVER or HOST PC as the authority. JOIN PARTY opens a list of open public parties (or type a code). Tick PUBLIC when hosting to show up in that list. Press Esc or the MENU button during a match to quit back to the menu.
